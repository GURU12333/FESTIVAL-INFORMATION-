Popular Indian Festivals

Discover the beauty and importance of Indian celebrations.

🪔 Diwali

The festival of lights celebrates the victory of light over darkness and good over evil.

🎨 Holi

The festival of colours celebrates happiness, friendship and the arrival of spring.

🌾 Pongal

A traditional Tamil harvest festival celebrated by thanking nature and farmers.

🎄 Christmas

Christmas celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ with joy, love and togetherness.
